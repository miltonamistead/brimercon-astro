Why membership
Mountain homes need a different kind of plumbing relationship.
In Truckee and Lake Tahoe, the gap between a small plumbing issue and a serious water-damage claim is
shorter than most homeowners expect. Freeze risk, seasonal vacancy, aging crawlspace systems, and the
distance from a trusted contractor all compound the exposure. A one-off service call is not the same as
having someone who knows your home.
Brimer Membership creates an ongoing service relationship, not a coupon program. Members receive
priority scheduling, preferred pricing, and regular plumbing visibility so small issues get caught
before they become expensive ones. For second-home owners and property managers, that continuity is
especially valuable.
For HOA boards and condo communities, the same model applies at building scale — a cleaner building-wide
protection strategy, a defined service relationship, and a single trusted contractor for water-damage
prevention across all units.
For HOA-managed properties
The same protection model, applied at building scale.
Brimer can structure building-wide service plans that combine recurring unit-level membership benefits
with project pricing for water-damage prevention upgrades, including Moen Flo installations and seismic
valves. After-hours emergency response is included as a member benefit for HOA properties. In many condo
communities, each unit has its own water main, meter, and plumbing conditions, so protection is often
needed at the unit level rather than only at the building level.
