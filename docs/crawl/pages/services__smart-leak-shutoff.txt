Water damage is the most common — and most expensive — insurance claim for mountain homeowners. A single undetected leak or frozen pipe can cause tens of thousands of dollars in damage, especially in homes that sit empty between visits.
Smart leak detection and automatic shutoff systems change that equation entirely. By monitoring your water supply in real time and shutting off flow the moment something looks wrong, these systems protect your home around the clock — whether you’re there or not.
Moen Flo Installation
We’re experienced installers of the Moen Flo Smart Water Monitor and Shutoff system. The device installs directly on your main water supply line and monitors flow, pressure, and temperature continuously. If it detects a leak, unusual usage, or freeze risk, it can shut off your water automatically and alert you via the smartphone app.
Installation is straightforward and typically completed in a few hours. We handle the plumbing connection, Wi-Fi setup, app configuration, and system calibration.
Freeze Protection
For Tahoe and Truckee homes, freeze detection is one of the most valuable features. The system monitors water temperature and pressure patterns that indicate freezing conditions, and it can shut off water before a frozen pipe bursts — even if you’re hundreds of miles away.
Remote Monitoring for Second Homes
If your Tahoe home is a second residence, smart water monitoring gives you visibility you wouldn’t otherwise have. The app shows real-time water usage, alerts you to anomalies, and lets you shut off water remotely with a single tap. Property managers can be added as secondary users for shared oversight.
Insurance Benefits
Many insurance providers now recognize smart water monitoring systems as a meaningful risk reduction measure. Installing a Moen Flo system may qualify you for premium discounts — and more importantly, it dramatically reduces the likelihood of a catastrophic water damage claim.
Common issues we resolve
Undetected Leaks in Vacant Homes
A small leak in an empty home can run for weeks, causing catastrophic water damage. Smart shutoff systems detect anomalies and close the main valve automatically.
Frozen Pipe Risk
When temperatures drop and a home is unoccupied, frozen pipes can burst without warning. Smart monitors track temperature and water pressure, alerting you before a freeze causes damage.
Insurance Requirements
Many insurers now recommend or require water monitoring systems for high-value properties. A Moen Flo installation can help meet those requirements and may reduce premiums.
No Visibility for Remote Owners
If you manage your Tahoe home from out of the area, a smart water monitor gives you real-time visibility into your plumbing system from anywhere.
Slow Leaks Behind Walls
Smart systems detect subtle changes in water flow that indicate hidden leaks — catching problems that would otherwise go unnoticed until visible damage appears.

Frequently asked questions
What is Moen Flo?
Moen Flo is a smart water monitoring system that installs on your main water line. It tracks water usage in real time, detects leaks and freeze risk, and can automatically shut off your water supply if it detects a problem.
Is this worth it for a home I only use part of the year?
Seasonal-use homes are where these systems provide the most value. A leak or freeze event in an unoccupied home can cause tens of thousands of dollars in damage. Smart monitoring gives you 24/7 protection and peace of mind.
Can my property manager monitor the system too?
Yes. The Moen Flo app supports multiple users, so both you and your property manager can receive alerts and control the system.
Does it work with well water?
Yes, Moen Flo works with both municipal and well water systems. We'll confirm compatibility during the consultation.
