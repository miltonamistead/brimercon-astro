Privacy Notice
This notice explains what information Brimer Plumbing collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Last updated: August 3, 2026
Brimer Plumbing ("Brimer," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Notice
describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit
brimercon.com (the "Site"), contact us, or request plumbing service. By using
the Site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.
Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you submit our service request
or contact form. This may include:
Your name
Phone number
Email address
Service or property address and city
Details about your plumbing issue, preferred timing, and any other information you choose to share
We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit the Site, such as your
IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring pages. This is collected
through cookies and similar technologies (see "Cookies & Analytics" below).
How We Use Your Information
To respond to your inquiries and schedule, perform, and follow up on plumbing services
To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request or appointment
To send service updates, review requests, and occasional offers (you can opt out at any time)
To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site
To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights
Text Messaging (SMS) and Calls
If you provide your phone number, we may call or text you about your service request, scheduling,
and account. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can opt out of
text messages at any time by replying STOP , or get help by replying
HELP . Opting out of marketing messages will not affect texts that are necessary
to complete a service you have requested. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or
affiliates for their own marketing purposes.
Cookies and Analytics
We use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to
understand how visitors use the Site and to improve performance and marketing. These tools may
collect information such as pages visited, time on site, and general location. You can control
cookies through your browser settings. To learn more about how Google uses data, visit
Google's Privacy Policy .
Service Providers
We share information with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our business, only as
needed to perform services for us. These include our customer relationship and messaging platform
(HighLevel / LeadConnector), our field service and invoicing software (Housecall Pro), and our
website hosting and analytics providers (Vercel, Google). These providers are authorized to use your
information only to provide services to us.
Google Maps Content
The Site may display business ratings and customer reviews supplied by Google Maps Platform.
Google may process technical information necessary to provide that content. Your use of Google
Maps content is subject to the
Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service
and the
Google Privacy Policy .
Review cards link to their source on Google Maps and identify how reviews are filtered and ordered.
How We Share Information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service
providers as described above, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights,
safety, and property of Brimer Plumbing, our customers, or others. If our business is involved in a
merger or sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
Data Retention
We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain business and
financial records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.
Your Choices and Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us
using the details below. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those emails,
and opt out of texts by replying STOP. Depending on where you live (including California residents),
you may have additional rights regarding your personal information; contact us and we will respond
consistent with applicable law.
Children's Privacy
The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from children.
Changes to This Notice
We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated"
date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.
