Gas Line Installation
New construction, remodels, and appliance additions often require new or extended gas lines. We size, route, and install gas piping to code — whether it’s for an indoor range, a tankless water heater, or an outdoor living space.
Every installation includes a pressure test and leak check before the system goes live.
Appliance Hookups
Adding a gas range, fireplace insert, dryer, or outdoor grill? We handle the connection from the gas supply to the appliance, ensuring proper sizing, safe routing, and code-compliant fittings.
Leak Detection & Safety
If you smell gas or suspect a leak, call us immediately. We use professional-grade detection equipment to locate leaks quickly and make safe, lasting repairs. For peace of mind, we also offer scheduled safety inspections — particularly recommended for seasonal-use homes before winter occupancy.
Shutoff Valves
Properly placed shutoff valves are a critical safety feature. We install and upgrade individual appliance shutoffs and whole-home emergency valves, giving you and your property manager clear control in any situation.

Frequently asked questions
How do I know if I have a gas leak?
Common signs include a sulfur or rotten-egg smell, hissing near gas lines, dead vegetation near outdoor lines, or higher-than-expected gas bills. If you suspect a leak, leave the area and call us or your gas utility immediately.
Can you run a gas line to my outdoor kitchen or fire pit?
Yes. We install gas lines for outdoor kitchens, built-in grills, fire pits, and patio heaters — all to code and properly sized for the appliance.
Do you perform gas safety inspections?
Absolutely. We offer comprehensive gas system inspections that cover all connections, shutoff valves, appliance hookups, and line integrity. This is especially valuable for second homes or properties that have been vacant.
