Whether it’s a dripping faucet, a slow drain, or a full kitchen remodel, the plumbing in your kitchen and bathrooms needs to work flawlessly — and the work itself needs to respect the finishes around it.
We serve homeowners and property managers across Truckee and North Lake Tahoe with careful, clean plumbing work that protects cabinetry, tile, and flooring while delivering reliable results.
Fixture Installation & Repair
From faucets and toilets to shower valves and tub spouts, we install and repair all standard kitchen and bathroom fixtures. Whether you’re upgrading to a new style or fixing a persistent leak, we ensure proper fit, function, and a clean finish.
Garbage Disposals
Disposal failures are one of the most common kitchen plumbing calls. We replace worn units, install new ones, and can advise on sizing and features that suit your household.
Drain Clearing & Repair
Slow drains and recurring clogs often point to buildup or pipe issues deeper in the system. We clear blockages and inspect drain lines to determine whether a simple clearing or a more involved repair is needed.
Remodel Rough-In
Planning a kitchen or bathroom renovation? We work with homeowners and contractors to plan and execute the plumbing rough-in — relocating supply lines, repositioning drains, and ensuring everything is ready for the finish phase.
Water Pressure Issues
Inconsistent or low water pressure affects comfort and appliance performance. We trace pressure problems to their source — whether it’s a valve, a regulator, mineral deposits, or undersized supply lines — and restore reliable flow.
Common issues we resolve
Dripping Faucets and Running Toilets
Beyond wasting water, these issues can signal worn valves or internal components that will only get worse. We diagnose and fix the root cause.
Slow or Clogged Drains
Buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains is common, especially in older mountain homes. We clear blockages and assess whether deeper pipe issues exist.
Low Water Pressure
Pressure problems can stem from supply lines, valves, fixtures, or mineral buildup. We trace the issue and restore consistent flow.
Leaks Under Sinks or Behind Walls
Hidden leaks cause damage to cabinetry, flooring, and framing. Early detection and repair protects your home's value and structure.
Remodel Plumbing Needs
Relocating fixtures, adding supply lines, or upgrading drain routing for a kitchen or bath remodel requires careful planning and clean execution.

Frequently asked questions
Can you help with plumbing for a kitchen or bathroom remodel?
Yes. We handle rough-in plumbing for remodels — including relocating supply and drain lines, installing new fixtures, and coordinating with your contractor on timing and access.
Do you install homeowner-purchased fixtures?
We do. If you've selected your own faucets, toilets, or fixtures, we'll install them properly. We're happy to advise on compatibility or quality if you'd like input before purchasing.
What causes low water pressure in mountain homes?
Common causes include partially closed valves, mineral buildup in supply lines, undersized piping, or pressure regulator issues. We'll diagnose the specific cause and recommend the right fix.
