Our story
Brimer Plumbing was founded in 1997 with a straightforward goal: provide the kind of plumbing service that mountain homeowners deserve — professional, clean, and honest. Over the years, that commitment hasn't changed.
We've grown alongside the communities we serve, from Truckee and Tahoe City to the smaller neighborhoods around Donner Lake, Olympic Valley, and the west shore. We understand the unique demands that altitude, cold winters, and seasonal use place on plumbing systems — and we build our work around those realities.
Whether it's a routine fixture replacement or an emergency burst pipe in the middle of January, our approach stays the same: careful diagnosis, clear options, clean execution, and verified results.
What guides our work
