Common questions
Frequently asked questions
What areas do you serve?
We serve the California side of Truckee and North Lake Tahoe, including Truckee, Tahoe City, Kings Beach, Tahoe Vista, Carnelian Bay, Homewood, Tahoma, Olympic Valley, Alpine Meadows, Dollar Point, Donner Lake, Tahoe Donner, Glenshire, and surrounding communities. We are not licensed in Nevada.
Do you offer emergency plumbing service?
Yes. For urgent issues like burst pipes, active leaks, or gas concerns, call us at 530-587-0733 for the fastest response. We prioritize emergency calls and can often respond same-day.
Do you work with property managers?
Absolutely. We regularly coordinate with property managers for seasonal homes, providing winterization, spring startup, emergency response, and ongoing maintenance.
What does your service process look like?
We diagnose the issue carefully, present clear options with pricing before work begins, protect your home's finishes while we work, and verify everything is working properly before we leave.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We are licensed plumbing contractors in California, fully insured, and have been serving the Truckee and North Lake Tahoe area since 1997.
