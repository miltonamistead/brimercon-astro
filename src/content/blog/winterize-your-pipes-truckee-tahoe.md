The cold nights are already here. Every winter, frozen pipes are the most common call we get in Truckee and Tahoe.

They are also the easiest problem to prevent. A frozen pipe can split and flood a home in a few hours. A little work now saves you all of that.

## 4 things to do before the first freeze

1. Take off your garden hoses. Water left in the hose freezes right at the wall and cracks the pipe.
2. Blow out your sprinkler lines. Just draining them is not enough at this elevation.
3. Wrap any exposed pipes. Check the crawl space, garage, and attic. Make sure your heat tape still works.
4. Find your main shutoff valve. Everyone in the house should know where it is and how to turn it.

## Leaving your Truckee home empty?

This is where the worst damage happens. Keep the heat at 55 degrees or higher while you are away.

Going away for a while? Have the water shut off and the lines drained, and ask someone to check on the place during a cold spell.

## If a pipe freezes in Truckee

Shut off the main water valve. Open the faucet so melting water has somewhere to go. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or a space heater.

Never use a torch or an open flame. If you see a bulge, a crack, or water where it should not be, call us right away.
